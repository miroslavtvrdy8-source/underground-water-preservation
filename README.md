# 🌍 Underground Water Preservation Website

A comprehensive educational website dedicated to raising awareness about underground water (groundwater) conservation and the importance of protecting our aquifers.

## 📋 Overview

This website provides information about:
- What underground water is and how aquifer systems work
- Why groundwater is critically important for human survival
- Major threats to our groundwater resources
- Practical solutions for water preservation
- Call-to-action initiatives for environmental protection

## 🎯 Features

✅ **Responsive Design** - Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices  
✅ **Professional Styling** - Water-themed blue color scheme with modern aesthetics  
✅ **Interactive Navigation** - Smooth scrolling between sections  
✅ **Educational Content** - Comprehensive information about groundwater  
✅ **Visual Cards** - Engaging card layouts with hover effects  
✅ **Statistics** - Key facts and figures about global water usage  
✅ **Call-to-Action Buttons** - Interactive buttons to engage users  
✅ **Mobile Optimized** - Fully responsive media queries
